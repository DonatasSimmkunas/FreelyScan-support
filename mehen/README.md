# MEHEN: Coil of Ra — Support

MEHEN: Coil of Ra is an Egyptian-inspired, offline strategy and bluffing game for iPhone and iPad, developed by Donatas Simkunas. This is the support page for MEHEN, not FreelyScan.

## Learn to play

Tap **? / How to play** from the home screen or during a match. The interactive lesson has seven steps in Duel of Ra and five in Reconstruction. You can skip, resume, and replay it. Practice is separate from your saved match. A searchable rulebook is available in Lithuanian and English.

Choose one to five hidden stones. If your opponent guesses correctly, they advance; otherwise you advance. Players switch roles after each turn. Reach the center exactly to win. Duel of Ra adds Ka, guardians, and special spaces. Reconstruction removes those modern powers. These modes are game adaptations, not a claim to preserve the exact ancient rules.

## Play offline or with another person

Play against the built-in AI or choose **Two players** to share one device. Use the handoff screen so the second player cannot see the hidden number. Internet multiplayer is not included. Core gameplay, both modes, all AI difficulties, guardians, and shared-device play are free.

## Pharaoh Collection

The optional Pharaoh Collection is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It includes two additional board styles, two additional soundscapes, Lotus and Scarab piece styles, Scribe and Oracle opponent appearances, and detailed local match statistics. Cosmetic choices do not change the rules, AI difficulty, or chances of winning. The purchase screen shows Apple's localized price when the product is available. A 30-second preview is available without purchase.

**Restore a purchase:** Open Pharaoh Collection from the home screen or Settings and tap **Restore purchases**, using the Apple Account that originally purchased it. An internet connection is needed for Apple's store services. If a purchase is pending approval, the collection unlocks only after Apple verifies it. There is no subscription to cancel. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

**TestFlight:** Purchases in a TestFlight build use Apple's sandbox, not a live purchase. Product availability also depends on the developer's App Store configuration. This support page does not claim that MEHEN has already been approved or released on the public App Store.

## Sound and saved matches

Music and effects have separate controls in Settings. Saved matches, preferences, lesson progress, and statistics remain on your device. Unrevealed secret choices are not saved. Deleting the app may remove local progress; restoring a purchase restores access to the collection, not deleted match data.
