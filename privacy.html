Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026.

Seller identification: VENT IT is currently operating in pre-launch order-request mode. Before card payments are enabled, the checkout will display the registered seller's legal name, registered address, company code and VAT number (where applicable). These details will also appear on the quotation/order confirmation.

What we collect

When you use VENT IT we may process account information, project data you save, floor-plan files you upload, order-request details, language/country preferences and limited technical event data used to keep the service reliable.

Why we process it

  • to provide the ventilation planner and cloud projects;
  • to respond to quotations and orders;
  • to provide customer support and delivery estimates;
  • to prevent abuse and diagnose technical errors;
  • to meet legal accounting, tax and consumer-protection obligations where applicable.

Analytics

VENT IT uses first-party, cookieless event logging for core product analytics and error monitoring. We do not intentionally place advertising trackers in the current release. Event logs are designed not to contain names, email addresses, postal addresses or payment details.

Service providers

We use infrastructure providers including Supabase for application data and Render for website hosting. Payment processing will be handled by a dedicated payment provider once card checkout is enabled.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data. Contact sales@vent.it.com.

Retention

We keep project and order data only as long as necessary for service delivery, support, security, accounting and legal obligations. Error and analytics event data is intended to be retained for a shorter operational period.

Security

Saved projects are protected with authenticated access controls. Payment card data will not be stored by VENT IT when third-party card checkout is enabled.
