Cookie & Local Storage Notice

VENT IT does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies in the current release. Optional first-party anonymous usage analytics are disabled until you consent.

Essential browser storage

We use browser local/session storage for language preference, Planner project drafts, cart contents, favourites, recently viewed products and an anonymous first-party session identifier for optional usage analytics only after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you choose “Essential only”, these analytics events are not sent.

Authentication

When you sign in to cloud projects, our authentication provider may use technically necessary storage to maintain the signed-in session.

Third-party services

Our infrastructure and future payment provider may use technically necessary cookies or storage to provide their service. If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced later, we will update this notice and add consent controls where required.
