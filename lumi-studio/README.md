## Create and save a look

Lumi Studio is a dress-up and character-creation studio. Explore the available items, change a character's look, and save creations in the local gallery. The project includes eight free stories. You do not need a developer account to play.

The iOS app includes its game files so that core studio play can work offline. Apple's purchase services and opening external support pages require an internet connection. A browser preview and the iOS app are separate versions; progress saved in one is not automatically transferred to the other.

## Missing saved work

Saved looks and preferences are kept locally. Confirm that a save has completed before leaving the app. Deleting the app or clearing a browser's storage can remove local work. Restoring a purchase restores eligible paid access, not deleted creations. Do not remove the app as a first troubleshooting step when you need to preserve a gallery.

## Optional purchases and parental controls

Where available in your app version and region, optional non-consumable content is purchased through Apple. The purchase screen must show the current localized price before confirmation. Complete the parent check to access protected actions. Apple may leave a purchase pending while approval is requested; content is unlocked after a verified transaction.

Use **Restore Purchases** in the parent or purchase area with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. In a TestFlight build, purchase testing uses Apple's sandbox and does not prove that a product is available for sale on the public App Store. A standalone browser demonstration does not complete real Apple purchases.

## Exporting a creation

Use the export action in the app. On iOS, protected export actions require the parent check and permission when saving to Photos. If export fails, check available device storage and the relevant permission in Settings, then try again. Keep the original saved look until you have verified the exported image.

## Reporting a problem

Describe what you expected and what happened instead. For purchase issues, include the product name, whether you are using TestFlight or the App Store version, and any error text. Do not include payment details. Apple handles billing and refund requests.
