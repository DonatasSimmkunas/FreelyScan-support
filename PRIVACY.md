# FreelyScan privacy policy

**Effective October 14, 2026.** This policy applies to the FreelyScan iPhone app published by its App Store seller.

## Documents and on-device processing

When you choose to photograph or import a page, FreelyScan processes the image on your iPhone to crop, enhance, read text, and create a PDF. Original images, processed pages, PDFs, saved signatures, annotations, recognized text for local search, and the running total of PDF bytes created are stored on your device. FreelyScan has no developer-operated document server or user account and does not upload the contents of your scans to one. Camera permission is requested by iOS when you use the camera; photo import uses the iOS photo picker.

## Sharing, deletion and backups

When you choose **Save or share**, iOS lets you send a copy to another app or save it in Files. The receiving app or destination controls that copy under its own terms. Deleting a document in FreelyScan removes its app-local document files and search text, but does not delete copies already shared or included in device backups. Deletion does not reset the cumulative free PDF allowance. To remove app-local documents, delete them within the app or remove the app, after exporting anything you need to keep.

## Purchases

Apple processes monthly and annual FreelyScan Pro subscription purchases. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to check your verified subscription status and unlock features. FreelyScan does not receive or store your payment card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) describes how Apple handles purchase information.

## Advertising technology

FreelyScan 1.0 build 14.1 does not include Google Mobile Ads or Google's User Messaging Platform, and does not request or display advertisements. These SDKs were removed from this build rather than only disabled at runtime.

Earlier 1.0 builds included these Google SDKs without live AdMob production identifiers; their release code did not start the ad SDK or request an ad without valid identifiers. If advertisements are introduced in a later version, we will update the app's disclosures and this policy before enabling that version. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) describes Google's handling of data in products using its services.

## Support requests

If you email support at [donatas@edvconsult.no](mailto:donatas@edvconsult.no), we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private scans or payment details unless specifically necessary. If you [open a support issue](https://github.com/DonatasSimmkunas/FreelyScan-support/issues/new), GitHub processes the information you choose to submit under its own privacy statement. Issues are public. Do not attach personal documents, signatures, passwords or payment information. We use the details you provide to answer or resolve your request. You can edit or delete your own issue through GitHub subject to its policies.

## Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the app or its services change, and will update the effective date. For questions about this policy or FreelyScan, email [donatas@edvconsult.no](mailto:donatas@edvconsult.no) or see the [support page](README.md). Do not send personal documents through a public issue.
