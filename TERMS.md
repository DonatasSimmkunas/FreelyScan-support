# FreelyScan subscriptions and terms

FreelyScan uses [Apple's Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) for the App Store app license. There is no separate custom EULA.

FreelyScan Pro is offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Both periods provide the same Pro tools. The App Store presents the price in your local currency and confirms it before payment. A subscription renews until canceled through your Apple Account, and access normally continues through the end of the paid period. The free app includes cumulative creation of 20 MB of PDFs on an iPhone; deleting PDFs does not replenish that allowance. Pro unlocks unlimited PDF creation, compression, organization, merge, page extraction, redaction, saved signatures, markup and custom scan profiles.
