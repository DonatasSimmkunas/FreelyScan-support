# Lumi Studio: Dress Up — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026.

Developer: **Donatas Simkunas**. Contact: [donatas@edvconsult.no](mailto:donatas@edvconsult.no).

This policy covers the Lumi Studio: Dress Up app for iPhone and iPad. It does not cover FreelyScan, Glimmi Salon or another app sharing this support repository. [Lumi Studio support](README.md).

## Your creations stay on your device

Lumi Studio does not require a developer account. The app includes its studio game files and runs its core character-creation and story features on your device. It does not operate a developer-hosted public gallery, messaging service or cloud-save service.

The app stores your gallery, draft, preferences, progress and family purchase setting locally. The native iOS implementation saves gallery state in the app's Application Support storage and uses local web storage for the included studio. These files are not sent to a developer-operated server by the app. Removing the app or clearing relevant storage can remove your saved work. Device backups and Photos synchronization depend on your device and Apple settings.

## Exports and backups you choose to share

A parent-authorized action can export a creation, save an image to Photos, or share a backup file through the system share sheet. The app requests add-only Photos permission when you choose to save an image; it does not require browsing your photo library to create a character.

When you select an external destination in the share sheet, that destination receives the content you selected and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Check the content before sharing it. The developer does not receive a copy merely because you export or save locally.

## Optional Apple purchases

Optional non-consumable content uses Apple's StoreKit services. Apple handles product, account, transaction and payment processing under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app reads verified purchase entitlements to determine which collections are available. The developer does not receive your payment-card details from the app, and the app does not send transaction records to a developer-operated purchase server.

Purchase and restore actions require the parent authorization in the app. The parent check is a locally evaluated arithmetic challenge; it is not identity verification or collection of a child's birth date. Restoring eligible paid access does not restore a deleted gallery. TestFlight uses Apple's purchase-testing environment.

## Advertising, analytics and tracking

The current implementation does not include advertising or third-party analytics services and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app's bundled privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking. Camera, microphone, location and contact access are not needed for its core studio features.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and diagnostic information according to the Apple services, settings and terms you use. This is distinct from developer-operated in-app analytics.

## Support correspondence

When you choose to email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond to your request and troubleshoot the problem. Parents and guardians should send only the information needed to describe an issue. Do not send a child's identifying information, passwords, Apple verification codes or payment-card details, and do not put private information in public GitHub issues.

Contact the email address above to request access to, correction of or deletion of support correspondence. Any applicable legal obligation to retain information still applies. The app's local gallery is on your device and cannot be recovered or deleted remotely by the developer.

## This support website

This page is hosted by GitHub. Visiting it is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Playing the bundled iOS studio does not require visiting this website.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Questions can be sent to [donatas@edvconsult.no](mailto:donatas@edvconsult.no).
