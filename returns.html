Returns & Right of Withdrawal

For consumers covered by EU distance-selling rules, a 14-day withdrawal period generally applies from delivery of goods. Statutory exceptions may apply, including clearly personalised or made-to-order goods.

Return shipping

Unless VENT IT agrees otherwise or the goods are defective/non-conforming, the customer normally bears the direct return-shipping cost where permitted by law. Large HVAC equipment can be expensive to return, so contact us before sending anything back.

Condition of returned goods

You may inspect goods as you would in a shop. A reduction in refund may apply where the goods have been handled beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning, where allowed by law.

How to start a return

Email sales@vent.it.com with the order number, item(s), reason and photos if relevant. We will provide return instructions.

Made-to-order/project items

Custom, configured or project-manufactured items may fall outside the standard withdrawal right. We identify such products before the order is finalised.
