# FreelyScan support

FreelyScan scans paper documents into PDFs on an iPhone. This repository contains [our privacy policy](PRIVACY.md), [subscription and license information](TERMS.md), and a public support channel.

## Contact support

[Open a FreelyScan support request](https://github.com/DonatasSimmkunas/FreelyScan-support/issues/new). A free GitHub account is required. Describe the app version, iPhone model, and what happened. **Issues are public:** do not attach scans, signatures, billing details, passwords, or other sensitive information. If you cannot use GitHub, use the support contact shown on the app's App Store product page once public distribution begins.

FreelyScan cannot read the PDFs stored in your app. If you are reporting a scanning problem, use a sample page without personal details.

## Common questions

**Where are my PDFs?** Documents stay in the app's local storage until you delete them. Use **Save or share PDF** to export a copy to Files or another app. Removing the app may remove its local documents; make copies of important scans first.

**Why does Pro appear when I tap a tool?** The app displays all Pro tools. A current monthly or annual subscription unlocks those tools. PDF creation is free up to a total of 20 MB on that iPhone; deleting a document does not restore this allowance.

**How do I cancel?** Manage your subscription through iPhone **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**, or tap **Manage subscription** in the app when Pro is active. Apple handles billing and refunds.

**How do I restore Pro?** Tap **Restore purchases** on the Pro screen while signed in to the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription.
