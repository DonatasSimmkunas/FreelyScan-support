# MEHEN: Coil of Ra — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. Developer: Donatas Simkunas. Contact: [donatas@edvconsult.no](mailto:donatas@edvconsult.no).

This policy covers MEHEN: Coil of Ra for iPhone and iPad, including its TestFlight build. It does not cover other apps in this repository. [Return to MEHEN support](README.md).

## Gameplay and local storage

MEHEN does not require an account. Its game logic, AI opponents, artwork, and synthesized game audio run on your device. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not send gameplay, match statistics, or lesson progress to a developer-operated server.

The app stores preferences, unfinished public match state, lesson progress, cosmetic selections, and match statistics in its local app storage. An unrevealed secret choice is not written into the saved match. Local data may be removed when you delete the app. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings; the developer does not operate a cloud-save service.

## Optional purchases

The optional Pharaoh Collection uses Apple's StoreKit services. When you request product information, buy, or restore, Apple's services process the request. The app reads Apple-verified purchase entitlements to decide whether to unlock the collection. The developer does not receive your payment card details from the app. The app does not upload transaction records to a developer-operated server.

Apple processes store, payment, and account information under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Purchase restoration needs the purchasing Apple Account and access to Apple's services. Restoring the collection does not restore deleted local match history. TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment.

## Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever information or attachments you send. This is used to respond to and resolve your request. Send only information necessary to explain the problem. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your support correspondence by contacting the address above; any legal requirement to retain information still applies. Do not submit sensitive information through public GitHub issues.

## Permissions and tracking

The current app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location. It does not use advertising identifiers or track you across other companies' apps or websites. There is no in-app chat, public profile, or user-uploaded content service.

## Apple diagnostics and this support website

Apple may collect diagnostics, TestFlight feedback, or store information according to the settings and terms you choose with Apple. This is separate from developer-operated in-app analytics, which MEHEN does not include. This support page is hosted on GitHub; visiting it is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The game does not require visiting this website to play.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Contact support with questions about this policy.

## Žaidimas ir duomenys įrenginyje

Paskyros kurti nereikia. Žaidimo logika, AI varžovai, grafika ir sintetinamas garsas veikia tavo įrenginyje. Programėlėje nėra reklamų ar trečiųjų šalių analitikos SDK. Partijos, statistika ir mokymo pažanga nesiunčiamos į kūrėjo valdomą serverį.

Vietinėje programėlės saugykloje laikomi nustatymai, nebaigtos partijos vieša būsena, mokymo pažanga, apipavidalinimo pasirinkimai ir statistika. Dar neatskleistas slaptas pasirinkimas į išsaugotą partiją neįrašomas. Pašalinus programėlę vietiniai duomenys gali būti prarasti. Įrenginio atsargines kopijas valdo tavo įrenginio ir „Apple“ nustatymai; kūrėjas neteikia debesinės partijų saugojimo paslaugos.

## Neprivalomas pirkinys

„Faraono kolekcijai“ naudojamos „Apple StoreKit“ paslaugos. Prekės informacijos užklausą, pirkimą ir atkūrimą apdoroja „Apple“. Programėlė tikrina „Apple“ patvirtintą teisę į kolekciją ir pagal ją atrakina turinį. Kūrėjas iš programėlės negauna mokėjimo kortelės duomenų, o sandorių įrašai nesiunčiami į kūrėjo serverį.

„Apple“ parduotuvės, mokėjimo ir paskyros informaciją tvarko pagal savo privatumo politiką. Pirkiniui atkurti reikia pirkimui naudotos „Apple“ paskyros ir ryšio su „Apple“ paslaugomis. Kolekcijos atkūrimas neatkuria ištrintų partijų duomenų. „TestFlight“ naudoja bandomąją pirkimų aplinką.
