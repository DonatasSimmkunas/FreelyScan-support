Shipping & Delivery

VENT IT is focused on deliveries from Lithuania to EU countries and Norway.

How the estimate works

The store estimates shipping using destination country, product weight and estimated shipment volume. Heavy or bulky items can receive an oversize/pallet surcharge. The final freight price is confirmed before payment.

Norway

Norway is outside the EU customs territory. Import VAT, customs clearance and carrier brokerage may apply depending on the shipment and customer status. These charges are confirmed in the final commercial offer where possible.

Lead time

Products may be in stock, available with a supplier lead time or made to order. Product availability shown online is indicative until confirmed on the order.

Damage in transit

Please inspect the shipment on delivery and notify us promptly of visible transport damage. Keep packaging and photographs where possible.
